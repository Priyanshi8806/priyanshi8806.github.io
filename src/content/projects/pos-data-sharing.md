## The 60-second version

When each company in a supply chain only sees the orders of the company below it, small wiggles in customer demand turn into big swings upstream — the *bullwhip effect*. Sharing sales data fixes much of this. But sharing has costs and the benefits mostly go upstream. So when will a retailer actually share? [Your answer.]
