At Kuehne+Nagel, I worked across road logistics and related operational functions, with exposure to client coordination, shipment planning, warehousing, and process improvement. Site visits and team interactions helped me understand how logistics decisions are made in practice and how operational complexity unfolds on the ground.

These experiences shaped my interest in operations, information systems, and data-driven decision-making by showing how real workflows depend on coordination across people, infrastructure, and technology.
