At Kuehne+Nagel, I worked primarily in Road Logistics while gaining exposure to other parts of the logistics network through operational visits and cross-functional interactions.

Working across road movement planning while observing sea logistics, air cargo, warehousing, and 4PL coordination helped me understand how physical operations, information flows, and managerial decision-making interact across a supply chain.

These experiences shaped my continuing interest in Operations and Information Systems.
